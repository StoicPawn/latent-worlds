# From theories to adversarial experiments

**Exploratory design document (2026-10-31).** No C1/C2/C3 data or
results are claimed. Any new outcome will require frozen preregistration,
code/data provenance and an independent held-out world-law sample.

## We are not testing the definition of consciousness

We test narrowly defined, theory-motivated computational organizations.
Passing does not imply phenomenal experience; a failure does not refute
phenomenal consciousness across all theories. A "proto-consciousness
score" is not calibrated or justified.

Our central experimental insight to pursue is NOT "make self-awareness":
**how easily can naive proxies of consciousness indicators be satisfied by
mechanisms that lack the theory's explanatory causal dynamics?** This is
the minimal-implementation / specificity problem discussed in Butlin 2026.

### Pre-experiment qualification checklist

Before any confirmatory condition:
1. Identify an actual information/decision opportunity accessible to
   the **agent**, not an all-knowing observer.
2. Ensure the experimental agent learns a task; if not, lesion-based
   changes are uninterpretable.
3. Equalize architecture resources, inputs, observed competence and
   intervention-induced action damage. Pilot matching is separate from
   held-out confirmation.
4. Log agent-accessible histories and external intervention identities;
   intervention labels may never reach the agent as rewards or oracle bits.
5. Freeze a mechanism-specific causal claim and a falsifying outcome,
   rather than merely testing whether "more complexity helps".

## Discriminating experiment D-HOT: metacognition vs convenience calibration

**Theory antecedent:** HOT-2/HOT-3, not an established consciousness test.

Matched internal recurrent fault (changes agent's own inference dynamics)
and external perception/world fault (changes task difficulty) may produce
similar immediate performance. Is information from the agent's PRIVATE
computational dynamics causally needed for different subsequent
repair/adaptation policies?

Counterfactual arms:
- self-internal-reliability route;
- equal-dimensional environmental/task-confidence route;
- equal-dimensional other-agent (same-time) route;
- constant/mean-input monitor and randomized semantic mapping;
- high-resource oracle used for diagnosing opportunity, never as an
  agent's privileged reward;
- untrained/supervised monitor as explicit emergence controls.

**Primary mechanism test:** remove only the private self route, and compare
future *external task reward* on held-out families of internal faults
versus external faults, beyond matched shams, same immediate action
disruption and fixed opportunities.

**Falsifies a strong endogenous-self-model interpretation if:** the
same effect is explained by task-confidence/logits or an equal-cost
external-only monitor; direct action wiring accounts for response; or
performance never learned enough to assess the mechanism.

**Limits:** even a robust win supports HOT-aligned metacognitive control,
not phenomenality. First-order auto-correction also can occur in
nonconscious systems.

## Discriminating experiment D-GWT: functional ignition vs broadcast copying

**Theory antecedent:** GWT-1..4; GNWT is not settled by AI behavior.

Agents possess independently useful task specialists. In a limited
workspace, only selected content should affect multiple downstream
modules through state-dependent routing, potentially across multiple
serial queries. Match compute, memory, information and parameter budgets.

Counterfactual variants:
- selective workspace with task-switching contingent attention;
- deterministic fixed winner of equal capacity;
- always-broadcast copied input;
- independent point-to-point routers of equal aggregate capacity;
- feedback/recurrent agent without global shared workspace;
- global bus that physically exists but is functionally ignored.

Primary: selective global task adaptation above matched routes, with
causal lesions of workspace selection and downstream availability.
Conclude NO GWT-specific evidence if simple copied/broadcast or
fixed-path architecture explains outcomes.

No formal inference from GWT functional implementation to experience.

## Discriminating experiment D-AST: attention self-model vs generic self-monitor

**Theory antecedent:** AST-1.

Directly perturb attention allocation (not just action outcome), measure
if an internal predictive attention-state model forecasts the
perturbation's effects and **causally controls attentional allocation**.
Compare equal-cost task uncertainty, static attention profile,
current-only monitor and time-shifted peer-attention models.
A generic internal error detector does not satisfy this theory-specific
mechanism, even if the word "self" appears in the code.

## Discriminating experiment D-PP: recursively predicted precision

**Theory antecedent:** PP-1, plus Laukkonen-Friston-Chandaria
"beautiful loop" proposal (not a new accepted AI consciousness test).

Compare two agents with equal external-world prediction:
one has an internally generated **global forecast of precision**
that feeds back into how prediction-error channels are weighted;
the other has a first-order confidence feature without recursive
precision forecasting. Also compare direct environment-driven gain
and non-feedback copied forecast with equal compute.

Selectively cut precision feedback, while preserving world-model
training and task performance. Measure causal adaptation under
unseen sensor reliability shifts, not mere prediction error.
A recurrent precision loop *may* be evidence for a theory-specific
computational architecture, not proof of minimal phenomenal awareness.
The theory itself explicitly says sophisticated self-modeling
may not be prerequisite for minimal awareness.

## RPT and IIT are not equivalent to the above

For RPT, test recurrent **perceptual** dynamics rather than a generic
RNN. The value of recurrence must survive matched feedforward depth,
delay, memory and specialist controls.

For IIT, *simulation variable entropy/graph connectivity/decoder
information* is not intrinsic physical cause-effect structure. A
digital simulator cannot by fiat validate substrate-invariant
consciousness. We will report such mechanistic findings as computational
facts; a substrate-sensitive theory may reach a very different
consciousness interpretation.

## Preliminary factorial designs

**Option A — minimal-implementation falsification (preferred initial
measurement contribution):** compare superficially identical GWT or HOT
indicator checklist passes in two architectures, one causally capable of
the required mechanism and the other a sham/copy. If both pass a naive
automated rubric but only one survives interventions, report specificity
failure of the rubric with false-positive rate and predeclared bounds.
This can be publishable as methods only if it improves on Chella 2026,
Dossa 2024, Tang 2026 and other near-duplicate work.

**Option B — emergent self-monitoring:** evolve equal-capacity
controllers with and without internal self-route access, under rewards
ONLY for survival. Test if a route develops functionally beneficial
causal self-modeling when facing previously unseen fault classes, across
independent physical worlds. Distinguish *engineered architecture
affordance* from *naturally selected use*.

**Option C — cross-theory dissociation:** two agents matched on reward
and visible behavior, one HOT-like self-monitor route and one
GWT-like selective broadcast, are separately lesioned. Compare
predeclared task/failure profiles that would be expected under each
computational theory. Without theory-unique predictions, do NOT
claim adjudication.

## Statistical unit and stopping

Report world-law clusters separately from population seeds, agents,
and timestamps. Predeclare a meaningful effect size, power design,
multiple comparisons across C1/C2/C3 and families of lesions.
Observer labels are for independent validation only. No selective
reporting of surviving lines, failed tasks or nulls.

A genuinely novel finding is **a rigorously isolated causal
mechanism or calibrated false-positive failure of an indicator proxy**,
not a claim that "emergence equals consciousness".
