# Research decision after literature audit — 31 October 2026

## What is no longer defensible

1. Equating a self-model with consciousness, or assuming explicit
   self-modeling is a universal first stage of consciousness.
2. Summing the 14 indicators into a "proto-consciousness score".
3. Renaming ordinary AI tools (confidence, recurrent memory, attention
   masks, workspace bus, simulated emotion, coherent social behavior)
   as new consciousness mechanisms.
4. Calling an interventional self-report, global workspace, or
   metacognitive-action link novel in isolation. Strong specific
   precedents exist through October 2026.
5. Treating a digital simulation as a validated test of the causal
   powers of a biological neural substrate or IIT phenomenology.
6. Rebranding the failed v5.4 ecology result or v5.5 pilot as direct
   consciousness evidence.

## What remains an experimental research opportunity

**Target:** assess the *specificity and causal validity* of
theory-derived COMPUTATIONAL indicators in an evolving, embodied-like
AI ecology that has survival reward but no consciousness objective.

**Mechanistic conjecture, not a fact:** apparently convincing indicators
can emerge as epiphenomenal task shortcuts or structural lookalikes
without their theory's proposed causal mechanisms. One can test this
by designing matched "naive indicator passes" and adversarial controls
that disambiguate causal routes under independent environmental laws.
Negative findings may be as valuable as positive ones *if sufficiently
powered, reported and generalizable*.

**No verified novelty yet.** Chella (2026) already proposes multi-theory
causal lesions, Dossa (2024) implements GWT architectures, Kumaran
(2026) shows causal confidence control and Tang (2026) shows
interventional causal-bypass mitigation. We must find an empirical
difference in *measured mechanisms* rather than merely claim an
integrated framework.

## Action sequence

**NOW — C0 (literature fidelity, not a new experiment):**
- 17 primary/conceptual sources, with evidence types and publication
  metadata; citation ledger SOURCES.json.
- The 14 original indicator IDs mapped to theories, dependencies and
  elementary counterexamples; BUTLIN_INDICATORS.json.
- Explicit comparison of human-neuroscience, computationalist,
  biological-naturalist and IIT arguments.
- Proposed adversarial experiments and clearly falsifying controls.
- Source status/research integrity test and deterministic Markdown
  renderer, included in CI.
- No phenomenal-consciousness scoring.

**NEXT — C0.5 (pre-experiment validity test):**
- Choose ONE theory-derived mechanistic property to probe, with a
  theory-specific countermodel and a predeclared false-positive test.
- Verify that both treated and control agents actually learn the
  task, that they have equal access to information/cost/compute and
  that relevant internal and external fault types are identifiable
  from agent-available trajectories.
- Freeze the mapping from proxy to theoretical mechanism and the
  perturbation family before any confirmatory test.
- Review latest literature anew before committing seed list and
  sample size. Existing v5.4 and v5.5 worlds are development worlds.

**FOLLOWING — causal study:**
- Cross-check at least two theory-specific alternative mechanisms;
  do not count correlated indicators as independent corroboration.
- Test lesion/site transfer and different physical-law worlds as
  distinct clusters, matched action-disruption costs, anti-bypass
  controls, observer-side positive controls and shams.
- Use confidence intervals at correct world unit, powered minimal
  interesting effect, and predeclare exclusions/dropouts.
- If the agent fails to learn an appropriate task, declare the
  experimental hypothesis **not assessed**, not refuted.

**PAPER GATE:**
- A clear novel theory-linked empirical mechanism or
  well-powered negative specificity result, beyond prior art.
- Predeclared null and competing theory predictions.
- External reward/causal use where relevant, not a probe AUC alone.
- Independent replication with meaningful uncertainty and complete
  code/data/figures and provenance.
- Correct statement: an indicator-aligned computational property
  is present/absent under tested conditions; no certainty about
  phenomenal experience.

## What not to do to chase publication

Do not tune the world until an attractive consciousness-looking
effect appears. Do not change reward to "desire awareness" and then
label its appearance spontaneous. Do not relabel familiar mechanisms
to make an apparent novel theory. Do not select only positive
seed histories or claim consciousness from an organized civilisation.

## Scope

CIVITAS ZERO remains genuinely about *AI consciousness science*
because the hypotheses are drawn from competing theories of
consciousness, and the measurements concern which computational
indicator mechanisms hold under intervention. The ecology is only
the stress-test. Even a successful paper would support the validity
of an indicator **relative to a theory**, not that subjective
experience has been generated.
