# Artificial consciousness: 2026 theory-first literature audit

**Cutoff: 2026-10-31.** Critical narrative review, not an exhaustive
systematic review. The reviewed primary sources are in SOURCES.json.

## The explanandum: consciousness is not just clever behavior

**Phenomenal consciousness**: subjective experience, "something it is
like" to be a system (Nagel, 1974; Block, 1995; Butlin et al., 2026).
**Access consciousness**: content available for reasoning, report,
memory and flexible action. **Self-representation**: representation
of one's body, attention, identity or computational processing.
**Metacognition**: monitoring and control of reliability of cognition.
**Agency**: goal-directed learning/action. **Sentience/valence**:
positively or negatively *felt* experience, not merely a numeric reward
or physiological-sounding energy variable. These properties are neither
mutually equivalent nor known to be jointly sufficient for phenomenal
consciousness. In particular, self-modeling need not entail experience.

"Proto-consciousness" has no agreed operational threshold suitable
for scientific grading. We will use "candidate computational indicator
relative to theory T", rather than inventing a proto-consciousness scale.

## Scientific accounts: their distinct commitments

### Recurrent Processing Theory (RPT)
Perceptual recurrent loops and organized integrated representations are
key features. A generic RNN update is not automatically the relevant
perceptual loop. Test selective disruption of perceptual feedback
against parameter/time-matched feedforward and working-memory controls.

### Global Workspace Theory / GNWT (GWT)
Multiple specialized modules compete to place content in a limited-capacity,
selectively accessed workspace, broadcasting it for *flexible downstream
use*. A software bus, duplicate representation or multiple classes
are weak proxies; distinguish selective access from fixed routing,
mere copies, and generic recurrence. Artificial global-workspace agents
have already been demonstrated (Frontiers Comp Neurosci 2024);
a workspace is not a new result. The Cogitate Consortium (Nature 2025)
pre-registered adversarial predictions in humans and found important
challenges to both GNWT and IIT. No uncontested theory winner.

### Higher-Order / metacognitive (HOT)
Metarepresentations or reliability monitoring contributing to belief
revision and behavior can be explanatory. Butlin HOT-2/3 require more
than a calibrated confidence score. In AI, Kumaran et al. (Nat Mach
Intell Sep 2026) already obtained causal evidence linking confidence
to abstention. Lindsey (arXiv Jan 2026) described limited functional
introspection; Tang et al. (arXiv Oct 28 2026) demonstrate causal
bypass and the effect of interventional training. Thus we cannot
claim novelty from a confidence-based decision or injected activation
being recognized. Counterfactual mechanism and out-of-distribution
generalization are indispensable, but those design ideas are also known.

### Attention Schema Theory (AST)
The proposed special property is a *predictive model of attention's
current state that enables control of attention*. It is not an arbitrary
monitor of internal damage or a generic hidden-state estimator.
Test theory-specific attention allocation, held-out changes and
causal selective lesion of the attention-control model.

### Predictive Processing / Active Inference (PP)
Predictive coding and endogenous precision regulation can be linked
to theories of consciousness. However, predictive coding itself is
widespread and not a sufficient test. The 2025 "beautiful loop" is
a theory proposal, not a demonstration of conscious AI. Test a
specified precision-control mechanism, not a generic predictive model
or a free-energy-sounding scalar.

### Agency, embodiment, interoception
Modeling sensorimotor action-observation contingencies is AE-2 in
Butlin's framework. Digital survival budgets, rewards, numerical
"pain", body schema and task performance do not establish valence
or phenomenality. Chella (Frontiers Psychol 2026) ALREADY integrates
multi-theory indicator profiles, causal lesions, sensorimotor/self-location
controls and psychological/ethical interpretation in a robotics context.
Merely combining such tests is a direct prior-art collision.

### IIT / biological-naturalist and substrate-sensitive alternatives
IIT emphasizes intrinsic cause-effect organization; external decoding
of information and simulated graph quantities are not inherently its
physical causal assessment. We must not label an arbitrary entropy,
integration or Phi-like score as proof of phenomenal consciousness.

Seth, Block and Tononi (Nature Comment, 19 Oct 2026) make a substantive
skeptical case that intelligence and standard silicon computation do
not establish experience. Schlicht (2026) and Koch (Neurosci Conscious
Sep 2026) question the transfer of biological indicators to AI.
This is a serious competing perspective, not a settled impossibility
theorem. Digital functional assays test *computational properties*;
the assumptions needed to infer subjective experience must be explicit
and remain unvalidated.

### Clinical tests and limits of inference
Bayne et al., Trends Cogn Sci 2024, discuss the validation problem
for tests of consciousness in humans and beyond. Butlin et al.,
Trends Cogn Sci (online 2025, issue June 2026), propose theory-derived
indicators that update credences, not necessary or jointly sufficient
features. The indicators can depend on each other (for example,
GWT-3/4 entail RPT-1 in their scheme), so counting them or assigning
an "8/10 consciousness" score is unjustified. Koch (2026) separates:
1) **calibration gap**: no independently verified artificial
phenomenal-consciousness ground truth; 2) **transfer gap**: a biological
indicator-consciousness relation need not carry over to digital systems.
Schwitzgebel (Cambridge, Aug 2026) offers a skeptical overview
emphasizing the substantial uncertainty.

## 2026 novelty vetoes

- Causal confidence use: Kumaran 2026 already shows it.
- Basic injected-state introspection: Lindsey 2026 already tests it.
- Interventional causal-bypass control: Tang Oct 2026 already develops it.
- Global workspace for embodied AI: an implementation exists since 2024.
- Multi-theory indicator + causal intervention framework: Chella 2026.
- Recurrent reservoirs and prediction selection: prior studies
  (including Tolle et al., Patterns 2026).
- Self-modeling, valence-like labels, recurrent loops or simulated
  societal behavior alone: no established consciousness claim.
- Count of 14 indicators: BUTLIN's original list, not ours.

## Experimental pivot: C0 before C1

**C0, mandatory theory-fidelity audit**: preserve all 14 indicator IDs
from Butlin, distinguish direct theoretical implication from an
exploratory engineering proxy, and construct a deliberately simplistic
near-null architecture for every indicator we intend to test.
Do NOT treat our C1/C2/C3 convenient assay groupings as theories
or automatically equate them to indicators.

**C1, self-versus-world perturbations**: supports a *candidate assay* of
HOT-2/3 and AE-2 only if the agent represents and uses reliability of
its own computations rather than generic environment or task uncertainty.
Include private-state ablation, action and norm matched shams, time-yoked
peer monitor, external-only monitor, confidence-only controller, and
an ideal observer diagnostic (not an agent). Competence and
agent-observable identifiability must first be demonstrated.

**C2, selective global availability**: assay GWT-2/3/4, under capacity
limits, competition, state-dependent routing, downstream flexible
reuse across independent specialist modules. Include copying and
matched local-routing control.

**C3, recursive monitoring / attention**: before coding choose
HOT-2/3, AST-1 or PP-1 target; each needs different interventions.
A general error monitor cannot be counted as all three.

**Cross-theory adjudication**: preregister predictions and null
cases that would favor different mechanisms rather than accepting
every possible observed effect. See THEORY_ADJUDICATION.md.

## Publication boundary

Possible genuinely new empirical contribution, NOT established:
show a **minimal-implementation failure and boundary conditions of
consciousness-aligned indicators** in a survival-evolved AI,
with matched competency and compute: systems that superficially
pass a structural/behavioral proxy need not exhibit the relevant
causal mechanism when tested with novel internal/external changes.

A prospective negative result may also be informative if false-positive
rates or equivalence bounds on a popular indicator proxy are powered
and independently replicated across physical laws. A pilot or
non-significant p-value alone is not such a result.

This would be a paper on **the validity of AI consciousness indicators**,
not an empirical proof of subjective experience.

## Citation policy

SOURCES.json lists publication and peer-review status and the DOI/link.
Treat preprints as preprints, opinion pieces as viewpoints, human
neural experiments as human, and interventions in language models as
evidence about the tested models. Refresh the review before
preregistration and before a manuscript is submitted.

## Addendum: critical direct collision found at C0.5 pilot start

- **Phua, Dec 22 2025, arXiv:2512.19155, preprint**: already constructs
  synthetic agent architectures to test GWT/HOT/IIT-like markers under
  targeted lesions. A self-model-off intervention that leaves first-order
  tasks intact but damages metacognitive calibration, workspace capacity
  lesions and unexpected broadcast noise fragility have been demonstrated.
  Therefore our C1 design cannot claim originality merely for the existence
  of separate internal representation, lesion and causal dissociation.
- **Butlin et al., Apr 25 2026, Trends Cogn Sci**, DOI
  10.1016/j.tics.2026.04.006: reply explicitly about consciousness
  indicators, mimicry, and internal variants. This strengthens the need
  to distinguish a proxy from the mechanism that allegedly justifies it.

Proposed C0.5 algebraically matched internal-weight versus external-sensor
perturbations in real Latent Worlds recurrent controllers are an
**identifiability calibration test**. Because their initial hidden state
is matched by deliberate construction while their raw sensory histories
are different, the pilot cannot establish endogenous self-awareness or
fully confound-free causal attribution. It is not a novel consciousness
indicator and not a publication-ready finding. See ../c05/PROTOCOL.md.
