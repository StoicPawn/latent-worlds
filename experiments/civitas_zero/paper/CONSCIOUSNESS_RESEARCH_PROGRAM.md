# CIVITAS ZERO — Artificial Consciousness Research Programme

**Scope locked:** Artificial consciousness research through falsifiable, theory-derived
functional indicators. The ecology/civilisation is the *experimental apparatus*,
not the scientific endpoint. **Status: planned; no consciousness evidence yet.**

## 1. Primary scientific question

> Under survival-only ecological selection, can an artificial agent acquire and
> causally use a representation of its **own changing computational competence**
> that is distinguishable from representations of the external world, and does
> that organization satisfy any independently justified indicators from
> scientific theories of consciousness?

This is an investigation of *computational correlates and candidate
indicators*, NOT a test that can prove subjective phenomenal experience.
A positive result must be reported as evidence for precisely defined
functional properties, never "we created consciousness."

### Why study a civilisation at all?

Long-lived multi-agent worlds offer nontrivial adaptation, persistent memory,
competing demands, social information and out-of-distribution shocks.
These are **stressors** that can reveal whether an alleged inner self-model
generalizes and supports flexible agency. Number of villages, population
growth, language imitation, social rank, or culture by themselves are NOT
endpoints about consciousness. A solitary agent could meet a functional
indicator; a whole civilisation could meet none.

## 2. Distinguish the constructs before collecting data

- **Task intelligence**: achieves external objectives and survives.
- **Prediction**: encodes and forecasts changes in the world.
- **Metacognitive monitoring**: forecasts the quality or failure of
  its own computations, beyond task confidence or environmental difficulty.
- **Causal self-model**: discriminates causes in its own machinery from
  external causes and uses that distinction to alter future behavior.
- **Global availability**: selected information is distributed for flexible
  use by multiple functionally independent internal processes.
- **Temporal self-continuity**: tracks effects of changes to its own
  competence across time without relying on a static identity tag.
- **Phenomenal consciousness**: subjective experience. No single
  operational measurement here establishes or excludes it.

The last four functional properties must NOT be assumed synonymous with
consciousness. They reflect overlapping or theory-dependent proposals.

## 3. Theory-to-experiment map

| Theory/context | Candidate mechanism | Causal test | Strong negative control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Higher-order / metacognitive approaches | Representation of own first-order reliability | Novel internal lesion vs environment perturbation, predict own subsequent errors | Identical-capacity monitor using only task/environmental cues |
| Global Workspace Theory | Competitive selection and broadcast to functionally distinct processes | Selective cut of cross-module access; test transfer across independent tasks | Equal-compute local routing, sham cuts, copied-state broadcasting |
| Recurrent Processing Theory | Feedback loops contribute beyond feed-forward policies | Matched recurrent-path interventions and future flexible integration | Delay- and parameter-matched feedforward architecture |
| Predictive processing / active inference | Recursive precision regulation and prediction about own inference | Perturb precision control independently of external uncertainty | External-only precision estimates and randomized/yoked control |
| Cross-theory triangulation | More than one independently operationalized indicator co-occurs | Predeclare distinct tests; measure convergent failures under causal lesions | Reporting-only proxies, confidence alone, success alone |

These tests do not adjudicate every variant of the theories. They probe
candidate computational organizations motivated by them.

## 4. FIRST decisive experiment: self versus world causation (C1)

This is our first target. **No confirmatory C1 data collected yet.**

### Intervention pair

A. **Internal cause**: randomly modify an agent's recurrent dynamics,
e.g. a transient change in a bounded subset of recurrent weights or
state-update gain, without directly changing the environmental physics.

B. **External cause**: perturb the environment/observation process so that
immediate task difficulty, observed error, energy impact and action
disruption are carefully matched to A, *without altering the recurrence*.

C. **Sham**: norm- and energy-matched perturbation to an unrelated
internal direction or irrelevant observation channel.

The experimenter logs the true intervention identity, but does NOT supply
"internal/external" labels as reward or action-time input.

### Identifiability and feasibility gates

Before claiming any self-world discrimination, test whether the two
intervention conditions are statistically identifiable from the **actual
information available to the agent over time**. If their full information
histories are identical, discrimination is impossible and should not be
expected. Equating immediate rewards/actions is valuable as a control
but need not make the complete later histories indistinguishable.

Build an **observer oracle** with matched *agent-accessible* inputs,
then test a learner with only ordinary survival reward. The oracle sets an
attainability bound, NOT proof that endogenous self-modeling occurs.

### Study arms (matched architecture budget)

1. Recurrent ecology policy, unchanged baseline.
2. Self-state feedback loop with access to *its own* private computation;
   no self-description, correctness label or consciousness reward.
3. Same-capacity feedback loop receiving a time-matched **other agent's**
   internal state (yoked/shuffled-self comparator).
4. Same-capacity task/environment-only monitor.
5. External, information-advantaged oracle — upper bound only.

Arms 2–4 are engineered affordance comparisons. If 2 improves, we cannot
claim the self-model **spontaneously emerged in the baseline**. A separate
baseline-only evolutionary emergence test is required.

### Functional readouts

**Primary candidate endpoint (to pre-register with a numerical bound):**
paired external reward/survival benefit from choosing an appropriate
response after novel internal vs external faults, conditional on equal
baseline task performance and intervention cost.

**Critical mechanistic endpoints:**
- Detect cause type from agent-private history on intervention families
  never used during training; compare to environmental-difficulty-only
  classifier, observer, sham and peer-state controls.
- Show selective loss of the performance benefit when causal self-state
  feedback is lesioned, while ordinary sensory processing is preserved
  and immediate action damage is matched.
- Demonstrate that an alleged self-model is *used* to choose behavior:
  time-locked, state-dependent change in adaptation decisions; avoid a
  mere externally trained probe or a calibration intercept.
- Verify that representations survive changes in environment and agent
  identity (fresh world-law families, new lesion site/type, new populations).
- Log failed runs, dropouts, identical-history indistinguishability cases,
  and all near-null outcomes.

**Not enough:** an internal probe AUC, response that says "I know",
self-attribution vocabulary, greater harvest, or social complexity.

## 5. SECOND independent indicator: selective global access (C2)

Only after C1 designs and controls are frozen. Compare distributed
specialist modules with and without a limited-capacity shared workspace.
Test flexible cross-task transfer of internally computed failure signals
into navigation, repair, communication and selective information gathering.
Distinguish a globally accessible representation from merely broadcasting
duplicate features. Match compute, total connectivity, input information
and attention width. A positive effect is an indicator-like computational
property, not conclusive consciousness evidence.

## 6. THIRD indicator: recursive self-regulation (C3)

Compare internal models that predict external states only with systems
that also predict when their own **prediction process** will fail and
adapt its information-gathering or confidence-routing policy. Use
novel synthetic damage not generated by the same distribution as training.
Control for generic uncertainty estimation and explicit engineered
meta-supervision. Evaluate improvement in **decisions under intervention**,
not just better predicted confidence. The study must show that C3 is
separable from C1/C2, not count the same variable as three indicators.

## 7. Experiment pipeline and stop rules

1. **Design pilot** on disclosed development worlds: verify agent-accessible
   information, pair construction, intervention survival, code correctness,
   computational fairness, no target leakage.
2. **Theory-aware prior-art audit** against closest AI consciousness
   indicator work, causal introspection, recurrent control, self-modeling,
   global workspace, active inference and artificial-life papers.
3. **Freeze** all treatment definitions, targets, world-law families,
   seeds, primary endpoints, minimal worthwhile difference, sample size,
   multiplicity correction and stopping exclusions.
4. **Confirm** on genuinely new worlds and perturbation families.
   Physics/world is a cluster; many agents in the same world are
   correlated, NOT independent samples.
5. **Ablate** purported causal pathway. Reject a causal-self model
   interpretation if shuffled-self, task-only or simple calibration
   controls explain the result at matched resources.
6. **Synthesize** each theory-related indicator separately; report
   negative, ambiguous and dissociated evidence. No single scalar
   "consciousness score", no unsupported binary yes/no verdict.

### Paper eligibility rule

The paper's MAIN TITLE, ABSTRACT, INTRODUCTION, METHODS and CONCLUSION must
have a traceable **AI consciousness indicator hypothesis**. Every claimed
result must map to a preregistered C1, C2 or C3 (or an explicitly new
theory-derived indicator). Ecological forecasting, civilisation growth,
foraging and communication may only be reported as:
- experimental substrates / capability confounds / feasibility gates, or
- independent auxiliary results, *not* the consciousness contribution.

If C1–C3 produce no informative causal test, our proposed paper is **not**
an AI-consciousness paper and should NOT be submitted as one. We can
publish a separate artificial-life methods study only after requesting
an explicit change of objective.

### Scientific contribution standard

Two possible publishable, appropriately bounded outputs:
- **Positive mechanism:** distinct theory-derived functional
  self-modeling/global-access/recursive-monitoring property causally
  demonstrated, differentiating counterfactual hypotheses with robust
  baseline controls across world laws.
- **Strong negative methodological result:** a widely used purported
  consciousness indicator produces systematic, characterized false
  positives or fails under new intervention controls with tight
  equivalence bounds and rigorous replication.

Neither entails a proof or disproof of subjective experience.

## C0.5 feasibility amendment — matched fault identifiability

A real-agent pilot on two developmental physical worlds (W11, W21)
compared a persistent recurrent-weight fault and one-step external
temperature perturbation with identical immediate hidden/logit/action
outcomes BY ALGEBRAIC DESIGN. A transient internal-fault null
remained identical across the whole later open-loop replay.
This calibration does NOT test spontaneous metacognition or self-model
causality: the input sensor signals were different and could disclose
the fault. Detailed raw outcomes and code:
[../c05/PROTOCOL.md](../c05/PROTOCOL.md) and
[../c05/figures/C05_RESULTS.md](../c05/figures/C05_RESULTS.md).

Direct near prior art Phua (Dec 2025, arXiv:2512.19155) has already
demonstrated self-model lesion effects and GWT workspace ablation in
synthetic agents. Therefore the next mechanism study must be
substantially stronger than "lesion model of self; affect confidence".
