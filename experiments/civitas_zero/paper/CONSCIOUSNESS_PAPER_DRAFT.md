## 1. Introduction

We test *computational properties motivated by theories of
consciousness*, rather than simply agent success or apparent social
complexity. The working operational core is a causal self-model:
an agent has access to and uses information that distinguishes changes
in its own inference machinery from changes in the external world.

We explicitly separate:
1. external world prediction;
2. first-order performance and confidence;
3. endogenous monitoring of one's own computation;
4. causal adaptation that depends on that monitoring;
5. multi-theory consciousness-indicator assessment.

Higher-order/metacognitive accounts, global-workspace accounts,
recurrent processing and active-inference perspectives suggest
different computational organizations. We will not force a single
composite "consciousness score" or treat any indicator as sufficient.

## 2. Candidate hypotheses AFTER the C0 literature gate (NOT preregistered yet)

H1: A distinct self-state route improves performance following
held-out *internal* mechanism faults above matched external-only,
sham and yoked-other-state controls, conditional on equal baseline
competence and costs.

H2: Selectively disabling the putative self-state route removes
that conditional advantage beyond matched generic action impairment.

H3: Any advantage transfers to new physical worlds, lesion families
and population histories, not only familiar ecological cues.

H4 (independent): Internal information can become globally
available to multiple specialist functions through a causal,
capacity-matched workspace, rather than coinciding with broadcast
of duplicate sensor features.

H5 (independent): A recursive precision/monitoring loop supports
adaptive response to loss of one's own reliability beyond first-order
confidence calibration.

H1–H5 are candidate functional tests, not statements about
phenomenological experience.

## 3. Methods to be implemented

- No explicit self-awareness reward or intervention labels to agents.
- Equal parameters, compute and environment information across arms.
- Internal versus external perturbations with verified identifiability
  from each agent's **legitimate** information history.
- Predeclared action-, reward- and energy-matched negative controls.
- Observer classifiers only as diagnostic upper-bound baselines.
- Held-out worlds as the physical-law unit of replication.
- Frozen inference/effect-threshold rules before confirmatory outcomes.
- Open-source code, seed tables, figure scripts and failing runs.

### C0.5 feasibility calibration — NOT a consciousness-indicator result

A deliberately algebraic matching of an internal recurrent lesion
and a one-step external sensor pulse produced identical immediate
hidden states/actions on 33 naturally evolved controller instances
from two developmental worlds; persistent lesions diverged afterward,
while transient repairs preserved matched paths. Both initial equality
and repair-control null are consequences of the intervention design.
The external arm has DIFFERENT raw sensory input and the continuations
are open-loop observation replays. No agent introspection, causal
self-use or adaptation has been measured. This result belongs in
methods feasibility, NOT as evidence for C1/C2/C3 or consciousness.

## 5. Interpretation hierarchy

- Detecting own state is **not** showing the state is used.
- Causal use of own state is **not** introspection in the human sense.
- Self-model plus global access is **not** proof of consciousness.
- Failure of a narrow functional property is **not** proof of
  absence of subjective experience.
- Claims must identify the precise theory, indicator, control and
  effect that the data support.

## 6. Publication decision

GO for a prospective mechanistic study of theory-derived
**AI-consciousness-relevant functional indicators** only if
a discriminating experiment is both original relative to prior work
and causally identified across independent physical worlds.

NO-GO for submitting a generic "civilisation emerged" paper under
the label of consciousness research.

## Theory-first amendment (31 October 2026)

The previous self-model-first sequence is too strong: explicit self-modeling
is not a universally accepted *necessary* precursor of awareness.
Laukkonen et al. (2025) propose that minimal awareness could occur
before richer self-modeling. We therefore precommit to a broader
theory-sensitive approach. The first deliverable is a faithful audit
of the 14 canonical indicators, not just our three C1/C2/C3
engineering suites. Distinct tests target GWT, HOT, AST, PP and RPT;
IIT and substrate-sensitive critiques remain an explicit limitation
on what a digital functional assay can conclude.
