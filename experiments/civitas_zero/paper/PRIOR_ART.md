## Candidate incremental research gap, NOT established novelty

A plausible empirical contribution would be a **paired ecological dissociation**
between (a) individual decodability of future environmental laws, (b) selection
of such information under pure survival pressure and (c) causal utilization
under action-preserving hidden-state interventions, across fresh world laws
without an epistemic training objective.

This *specific* conjunction and its empirical boundary conditions must be
screened against artificial-life, ecological rationality, reservoir computing,
causal probing, self-intervention and developmental learning papers. An exact
string mismatch in literature search is not evidence of novelty.

## Claim vetoes

- Any observed higher decoder AUC alone: **NO discovery claim**.
- Any positive result without held-out worlds: **NO universal knowledge claim**.
- Any societal-looking behavior hard-coded as a reward: **NO spontaneous civilisation claim**.
- Any report of self, guilt, introspection or emotion by an artificial agent:
  **NO phenomenal consciousness inference**.
- Any candidate selected or tuned on confirmatory seeds: **NO prospective confirmation**.

dissociations** and new world/lesion generalization, not new terminology.

## Strongest newly found synthetic consciousness marker collision — Oct 2026 audit

**Phua (2025), arXiv:2512.19155**, *Can We Test Consciousness Theories
on AI? Ablations, Markers, and Robustness* (preprint), already uses
three controlled synthetic agent experiments. A self-model lesion
degrades metacognitive calibration while leaving first-order task
performance intact; workspace lesions cause changes in access-related
markers; broadcasting amplifies noise; perturbational complexity
does not behave naively. An "AI indicator can fail under a lesion"
contribution is a near-duplicate, not a new discovery.

**Butlin et al. (2026), DOI 10.1016/j.tics.2026.04.006**, *Consciousness
indicators, mimicry, and internal variants* — concerns about formal
indicator mimicry and mechanistic heterogeneity are acknowledged in
the original assessment programme.

The C0.5 internal/external algebraic match is a *calibration fixture*
whose immediate equality is guaranteed by construction and whose
sensors differ. It is explicitly not submitted as a scientific
discovery or a consciousness result.
