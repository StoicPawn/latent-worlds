## Candidate incremental research gap, NOT established novelty

A plausible empirical contribution would be a **paired ecological dissociation**
between (a) individual decodability of future environmental laws, (b) selection
of such information under pure survival pressure and (c) causal utilization
under action-preserving hidden-state interventions, across fresh world laws
without an epistemic training objective.

This *specific* conjunction and its empirical boundary conditions must be
screened against artificial-life, ecological rationality, reservoir computing,
causal probing, self-intervention and developmental learning papers. An exact
string mismatch in literature search is not evidence of novelty.

## Claim vetoes

- Any observed higher decoder AUC alone: **NO discovery claim**.
- Any positive result without held-out worlds: **NO universal knowledge claim**.
- Any societal-looking behavior hard-coded as a reward: **NO spontaneous civilisation claim**.
- Any report of self, guilt, introspection or emotion by an artificial agent:
  **NO phenomenal consciousness inference**.
- Any candidate selected or tuned on confirmatory seeds: **NO prospective confirmation**.
