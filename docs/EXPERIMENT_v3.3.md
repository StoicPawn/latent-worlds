# v3.3 — Staged search for adaptive replication

No agent/world dynamics changed in this release.

## Pre-registered order

1. G2+ must actively emit and receive.
2. Same-agent signal removal must support a causal copy effect.
3. Real-payload transplant must steer outgoing content.
4. Emergent variants must have different matched-context reproduction rates.
5. The corresponding scrambled-evolution history must *not* support the removal effect.
6. Only then are readable/censored/scrambled fitness controls evaluated.

## New-world campaign

Worlds 34–49 yielded one late-communication candidate, W42, which failed removal
and transplant. Worlds 50–81 yielded W56/W63/W65 plus later W70/W72/W78/W80.
W56, W65, W70, W72 and W78 failed the direct removal criterion or lacked enough
natural G2 broadcast events. W80 passed removal, transplant and differential
reproduction, but scrambled evolution also passed removal; W80 is therefore a
non-specific false positive under the v3.3 criterion.

### W63 × A0

At 800 steps, assay window starting at 450, G2:

- removal: 88 receptions, 70 natural broadcasts, mean copy gain ~ +0.0324,
  positive fraction 1.0, permutation p=0.01;
- transplant: supported in G2;
- matched-context reproduction: three variants, reproduction rates
  0 / 0 / ~0.795, strong differential reproduction;
- scrambled-evolution removal: unsupported (zero late assay events in the matched window).

Thus W63 is an independent **content-specific mechanistic replication**.

Fitness, however, is not adaptively replicated. Readable minus censored harvest:

- t=500: -119.87
- t=650: -154.82
- t=800: -203.92

Readable minus scrambled harvest:

- t=500: +42.13
- t=650: +66.49
- t=800: +95.28

Readable content is therefore more effective than destroyed content but remains
net-costly relative to censorship in this history.

Worlds 90–105 produced W90 and W104 as late-generation communication candidates;
both failed because G2 did not naturally rebroadcast in the causal assay window.

## Current status

We now have multiple independent mechanistic histories (W11, W21, W63) but only
W11 shows persistent adaptive superiority over both censorship and scrambling.
The target remains an **independent persistent adaptive replication** before any
claim of a stable second evolutionary process is justified.
