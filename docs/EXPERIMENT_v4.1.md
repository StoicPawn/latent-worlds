# v4.1 — Full persistent-adaptive replication in robust ecological cells

## Scientific target

This experiment is preregistered before the v4.0 result is inspected.

The first high-value target for the current branch is a **second independent history** that satisfies the complete non-genetic inheritance criterion rather than merely showing communication or a direct copy effect.

A qualifying history must satisfy all of the following under the existing observer-side assays:

1. late-generation (G2+) communication;
2. direct same-agent causal copy effect under signal removal;
3. content-specific steering under real-payload transplantation;
4. matched-context differential reproduction of signal variants;
5. failure of the corresponding mechanism under scrambled-content evolution;
6. readable fitness above cost-matched censorship;
7. readable fitness above payload scrambling;
8. persistence of the adaptive advantage across preregistered horizons.

No agent capability, objective, reward, learning rule, reproduction rule, communication primitive or physical law may be changed to obtain a positive result.

## Dependency on v4.0

v4.0 tests whether positive v3.9 ecological cells generalise across independent population seeds. v4.1 only uses cells selected by a deterministic rule from the committed v4.0 result.

Selection rule:

- consider only cells with `population_replicates >= 2` among A1–A4 in addition to A0;
- rank eligible cells by replicate count, then by lower signal cost, then by higher active-obstacle fraction;
- take at most the first three cells;
- within each selected cell, test population seeds A1–A4 that showed direct-removal support in v4.0;
- A0 is not allowed to count as the required independent replication.

If v4.0 contains no cell meeting the replicate threshold, v4.1 records a negative result and performs no post-hoc cell substitution.

## Claim boundary

A positive v4.1 history would support a substantially stronger statement than the current W11 result alone: the complete persistent-adaptive mechanism can recur in an independently initialised population within a preregistered ecological regime.

It would still **not** by itself prove open-ended culture, language, cumulative epistemology, or spontaneous science. Those remain later steps toward the North Star.
