# v4.2 — Historical-contingency expansion

## Motivation

v4.0 found no preregistered supported v3.9 cell that reproduced direct causal transmission in A1–A4, but one preregistered boundary control produced an independent A2 hit. This is evidence for strong world × history interaction, not for a population-robust ecological basin.

The next experiment must not promote that single A2 observation directly to a strong replication claim. Instead, v4.2 tests whether independent origins occur at a measurable rate when population history is varied more broadly under conditions fixed before the new runs.

## Primary question

**Can direct causal non-genetic transmission re-emerge independently, at a rate above matched boundary controls, when only population history is changed?**

## No agent-side changes

No capability, objective, reward, learning rule, sensor, action, inheritance mechanism, communication primitive, or physical law is added or modified.

## Preregistered design

Use W11 and exactly four ecological conditions chosen from the already committed v4.0 result:

1. the boundary condition `(active_obstacle_fraction=0.5, signal_cost=0.03)` that produced the independent A2 discovery;
2. the canonical positive A0 condition `(1.0, 0.03)`;
3. the nearest v4.0 boundary control with no independent support `(0.75, 0.03)`;
4. the v4.0 boundary control `(1.0, 0.04)`.

Evaluate new population seeds **A5–A24** only. A0–A4 are discovery/history data and cannot count toward the v4.2 confirmatory result.

For every condition × population seed, run the unchanged direct same-agent reception-removal assay with the same turnover regime, 650 steps, assay start 250, G2+ criterion, information level 0.35, and 99 permutations.

## Primary endpoint

For each condition, estimate the independent-origin rate among A5–A24. The primary comparison is the A2-discovery condition `(0.5, 0.03)` versus the two preregistered negative boundary controls.

A condition is called **history-reproducible** if at least 3 of 20 new population seeds support G2+ direct causal transmission. This threshold is fixed before A5–A24 are run.

## Secondary endpoint

Any newly positive A5–A24 history is a candidate for the existing full mechanistic/adaptive follow-up, but the full assay must be reported separately and cannot retroactively alter the v4.2 endpoint.

## Claim boundary

A positive v4.2 would establish that the phenomenon is not unique to A0 or the discovered A2 history and that independent origins recur under fixed world-side conditions. It would not yet establish persistent-adaptive secondary evolution; that requires the full causal, content-specific, scrambling, fitness, and persistence criteria.
