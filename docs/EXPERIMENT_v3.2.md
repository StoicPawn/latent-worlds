# Experiment v3.2 — Adaptive persistence and independent-replication search

## Question
Can the direct-causal non-genetic transmission mechanism replicate independently **and** confer an adaptive advantage that persists across preregistered horizons?

No agent objective, reward, cognitive primitive, communication rule, or physics law was added in v3.2.

## Independent-replication screening
Using the standard turnover world-side profile, worlds 22–33 with population seed A0 were screened at 800 steps for late-generation communication. None produced a new G2+ communication-active history suitable for the full v3.1 causal stack. Several reached G2/G3 genealogically, but late generations emitted too little.

Additional population seeds were screened in the previously interesting worlds W11 and W21. W11×A2, W11×A3 and W21×A1 had enough nominal G2 activity to merit a direct reception→transmission test. All three failed the first causal criterion:

- W11×A2: 61 G2 receptions, 0 natural G2 broadcasts.
- W11×A3: 193 G2 receptions, 17 natural broadcasts; mean copy gain ≈ +0.0055 but permutation p ≈ 0.98.
- W21×A1: 54 G2 receptions, 4 natural broadcasts; insufficient causal support.

They were therefore not promoted to transplant/selection follow-up.

## Temporal adaptive persistence of the canonical candidate
The original W11×A0 history was evaluated at fixed horizons 500, 650 and 800 using identical readable, censored and scrambled conditions.

| Horizon | Harvest readable − censored | Harvest readable − scrambled | Population readable − censored | Population readable − scrambled |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 500 | +76.20 | +174.33 | +1 | +8 |
| 650 | +69.17 | +247.24 | 0 | +10 |
| 800 | +86.83 | +372.53 | +2 | +12 |

The conservative adaptive criterion is satisfied at all three horizons: readable content beats both controls on cumulative harvest and never loses final population to either.

This is **temporal replication within one history**, not an independent-history adaptive replication.

## Current interpretation
Two claims remain separated:

1. **Mechanistic independent replication:** already observed in W11×A0 (G2) and W21×A0 (G3).
2. **Adaptive independent replication:** not yet observed. W11×A0 shows persistent adaptive advantage across horizons, while the independent mechanistic replicate W21×A0 did not beat scrambling on harvest.

Thus the next target is a new world×population history that satisfies both the direct-causal triad and the preregistered multi-horizon adaptive criterion.
