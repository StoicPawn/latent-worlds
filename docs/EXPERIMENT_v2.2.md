# Experiment v2.2 — Hierarchical joint search

## Principle
No new agent-side capability, reward, semantic primitive, or physics law is introduced. The goal is to purchase more evolutionary history and search efficiently for the rare intersection of genealogical depth and content-sensitive communication.

## Pre-registered stage 1
A history advances only if max generation >= 2 and final population >= 8. Screening varies world seed, independently seeded initial population, and a small set of purely demographic/ecological profiles.

## Pilot
2 worlds x 2 populations x 2 profiles = 8 histories at 450 steps. Four histories reached G2 while remaining non-collapsed. High-turnover ecology increased births but did not guarantee G2 (e.g. world 1 x population 0 remained G1 despite 40 births), reinforcing world-population-history interaction.

## Stage 2
Only stage-1 candidates receive expensive counterfactual semantic autopsy: real payload evolution versus scrambled-payload evolution, with minimum sample floors by generation. The semantic assay is intentionally not weakened to fit compute constraints.

## Interpretation
Genealogical depth and semantic structure remain separate endpoints. More generations alone are not evidence of culture; content sensitivity alone is not evidence of fixation. A serious candidate must satisfy both and ultimately show fitness advantage under causal controls.
