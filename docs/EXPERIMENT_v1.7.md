# Experiment v1.7 — Birth, extinction and non-fixation of proto-conventions

## Principle
Version 1.7 adds no agent faculty, reward, semantic prior or world mechanism. It only expands the observer-side campaign and matched causal follow-up.

## 24-world strict screen
Three fragmentation regimes (`q=0.35, 1.0, 1.65`), eight seeds each, signal cost `0.03`, 800 steps, 100-step observer windows.

Strict cross-generational burst candidates survived in 5/24 worlds:
- q=0.35 seed=0: t=400–600, 3 windows, up to 3 active signalling generations.
- q=0.35 seed=1: t=400–600, 3 windows, up to 2 generations.
- q=0.35 seed=7: t=300–500, 3 windows, up to 2 generations.
- q=1.0 seed=7: t=100–500, 5 windows, up to 2 generations.
- q=1.65 seed=1: t=400–600, 3 windows, up to 2 generations.

## 1,600-step readable-vs-censored follow-up
All five strict bursts disappeared rather than fixing. Matched communication effects on cumulative harvest were:
- q=0.35 seed=0: 2455.9 vs 2672.7, delta -216.8; population 21 vs 25.
- q=0.35 seed=1: 2065.9 vs 2571.1, delta -505.2; population 15 vs 20.
- q=0.35 seed=7: 1036.8 vs 1079.3, delta -42.5; population 2 vs 1.
- q=1.0 seed=7: 1143.1 vs 1412.8, delta -269.7; population 2 vs 3.
- q=1.65 seed=1: 2345.2 vs 2329.8, delta +15.4; population 17 vs 17.

The final regime was extended to 3,000 steps. Its early burst still did not reappear or deepen: readable population 2 vs censored 3, cumulative harvest 3106.0 vs 3063.2, maximum generation 1 in both histories.

## Interpretation
The strongest current pattern is not language fixation. It is the spontaneous birth of informational sender/receiver structure that can span generations and then disappear. Most strict bursts are costly or maladaptive under matched censoring; one is approximately neutral on intermediate horizons. This motivates a birth–death–fixation framing rather than a language-success framing.

A future fixation claim requires persistent null-aware encoding and uptake after the original burst, increasing generational depth, and a reproducible positive matched causal effect on population-level fitness.
